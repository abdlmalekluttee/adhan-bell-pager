# Arabic glossary

Use these terms consistently in `app/static/js/locales/ar.js`. Register: clear Modern Standard Arabic for school and
mosque staff; short labels for buttons, full sentences for explanations. Keep `{placeholders}` unchanged. Keep product
and protocol names in Latin letters where staff know them that way (SIP, PBX, LDAPS, StartTLS, RTP, NTP, UDP, TCP,
TLS, G.722, G.711, Opus, Active Directory). Numbers are shown with Western digits (0–9).
